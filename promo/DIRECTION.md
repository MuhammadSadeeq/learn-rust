# OXIDIZE: the launch film

**Director's treatment, style guide, storyboard, engine contract and score.**

A 64-second, 1920×1080, 60 fps showcase film for Oxidize. Every frame and every note is generated by code in this folder: HTML/SVG/CSS rendered frame by frame in Chromium, and music synthesized sample by sample in numpy.

`timing.json` is the cue sheet. Visuals and music both read it.

---

## 1. The idea

> **Rust isn't hard. It's invisible.**

Rust's rules (ownership, borrowing, lifetimes) govern things you can't see. That is the whole pitch of Oxidize: it makes them visible.

The film enacts that pitch:
1. It opens in the **invisible world**: raw text on black, a compiler that says no, errors piling up like a wall.
2. One hammer blow on the downbeat **forges the logo**.
3. From then on everything is **seen**:
   - a crab holds a leash to its value;
   - the leash passes on a move;
   - the Inspector stamps a borrow error;
   - a Vec's values hop to a bigger plot.
4. Then it shows the **proof**: a real terminal, a real compiler, a boss whose HP drains with every test that passes.
5. Then the **journey** across the island, and the **payoff**: you end up building an AI agent harness in Rust.

**The emotional arc:**
- 0–16 s: tension (dark, tight, red);
- 16 s: release (a molten orange forge strike);
- 16–56 s: joy, clarity and momentum (warm, rhythmic, playful);
- 56–64 s: pride (a big resolved chord, the logo).

**The one rule the film must keep, because it's the product's promise:**
- Every picture carries its **real Rust term**.
- The crab is labelled as a variable, the leash as ownership, the Inspector as the borrow checker.
- The fun never replaces the words.

## 2. Style guide

**Palette.** The CSS variables live in `engine.js`. Use them, never ad-hoc hex values.

| token | hex | use |
|---|---|---|
| `--ink` | `#0b0d10` | background base |
| `--ink-2` | `#151920` | panels, raised background |
| `--steel` | `#2a313b` | lines, panel borders |
| `--oxide` | `#e8590c` | brand, owners, emphasis |
| `--hot` | `#ff8a3d` | glow, highlights |
| `--molten` | `#ffd38a` | hottest metal, sparks |
| `--cream` | `#fff4e3` | main text on dark |
| `--muted` | `#9aa3ad` | secondary text |
| `--borrow` | `#4c8dff` | shared borrows (`&`) |
| `--lock` | `#c084fc` | exclusive borrows (`&mut`) |
| `--error` | `#ff4d4d` | errors |
| `--ok` | `#3ad07a` | success |
| `--ghost` | `#6b737d` | moved-from values |

**Type.** The fonts are local files in `fonts/`.
- **Display:** *Sofia Sans Extra Condensed* 800/900, uppercase, tracking 0.02em. Hero 220–300 px; section words 160–200 px; captions 64–96 px.
- **Code:** *JetBrains Mono* 500/700, 36–48 px in hero code, 26–32 px in terminals.
- **Body:** *Atkinson Hyperlegible Next* 400/700, 36–56 px for subtitles.
- **Safe area:** text stays inside 96 px left and right, and 72 px top and bottom.

**Motion grammar.** These rules are non-negotiable.
1. **Nothing pops without motion.** Every entrance has easing, usually `outBack` (playful things) or `outExpo` (type), over 300–600 ms.
2. **Exits are faster than entrances:** `inQuad`/`inExpo`, 180–350 ms.
3. **Hard cuts land exactly on a cue** (a downbeat), never between.
4. **No dead frames.** During a hold, everything keeps a slow drift: scale 1 → 1.03, or a 4–8 px float. Each scene also has its own slow camera push.
5. **Stagger:** letters 18–30 ms; words 80–120 ms; list items 120–160 ms.
6. **Cause before effect.** The hammer hits, then the sparks fly. The leash travels, then the old crab turns grey.
7. **Impacts get a 6–14 px screen shake** that decays over 250–400 ms, from `V.shake`. Only impacts that are in the cue sheet get one.
8. **Depth:** a soft vignette and film grain on every frame (the engine provides them), plus subtle parallax in busy scenes.
9. **Characters come from the course itself** (`OxCast.el`, `OxCast.boss`), at 140–240 px, with a soft shadow below. CSS animation is disabled in the film, so all life (hops, bobs, poses) comes from `render(t)`.

## 3. Storyboard

Global times in seconds; `@` means a cue from `timing.json`.

### S1 · INVISIBLE (0–8)
- **Background:** black (`--ink`), grain. The camera slowly pushes in, scale 1.00 → 1.06 over the scene.
- **0.0–0.6:** an orange block cursor blinks (on 250 ms, off 250 ms) at the code's start position, left-center.
- **@typeStart 0.6 → @typeEnd 3.2:** typewriter, in JetBrains Mono 46 px, cream:
  ```rust
  let name = String::from("Ana");
  let other = name;
  println!("{name}");
  ```
  Use syntax colors: keywords oxide, strings `--molten`, macros `--borrow`. The caret follows the typing.
- **@errorFlash 3.5:** a red wavy underline draws under `{name}` on line 3. Below it a compiler block slides up 24 px and fades in (mono 30 px):
  `error[E0382]: borrow of moved value: \`name\``
  "error[E0382]" is red, and the message is cream. Add a small 6 px shake and a 10 % red flash over the screen.
- **@textHard 4.0:** the code dims to 35 %. Center-upper: **RUST ISN'T HARD.** in display 180 px, cream. Letters rise 40 px with `outExpo` at a 22 ms stagger.
- **@textInvisible 6.0:** below it, **IT'S INVISIBLE.** in display 180 px, oxide, with the same entrance.
- **@dissolve 7.0 → 8.0:** the letters of "IT'S INVISIBLE." fade out one by one in a seeded random order, each blurring 0 → 8 px as it goes. The code fades to 8 %. By 8.0 only the faint code is left.

### S2 · THE WALL (8–16)
- **@errorCards** (12 hits, on the beat): each hit slams in one compiler-error card.
  - **The card:** `--ink-2` panel with a 6 px red left bar, mono 30 px, `error[EXXXX]:` in red plus its message in cream.
  - **Placement:** seeded random over the screen, rotation ±4°, never covering the center 900×260 px box (the giant words go there).
  - **Entrance:** scale 1.18 → 1 and opacity 0 → 1 over 120 ms (`outQuad`), plus a 6 px screen shake.
  - **Mood:** the background warms toward dark red (`#2a0b0b`) as cards accumulate. Older cards dim to 60 %.
  - **The cards, in order, must be real rustc 1.97 messages.** Verify each one by compiling a tiny program, and keep the variable names short:
    - E0382 borrow of moved value: `name`
    - E0499 cannot borrow `v` as mutable more than once at a time
    - E0502 cannot borrow `v` as mutable because it is also borrowed as immutable
    - E0597 `x` does not live long enough
    - E0106 missing lifetime specifier
    - E0505 cannot move out of `s` because it is borrowed
    - E0507 cannot move out of `*r` which is behind a shared reference
    - E0384 cannot assign twice to immutable variable `x`
    - E0373 closure may outlive the current function, but it borrows `v`, which is owned by the current function
    - E0716 temporary value dropped while borrowed
    - E0277 `Rc<i32>` cannot be sent between threads safely
    - E0308 mismatched types
- **@wordHits 10, 12, 14:**
  - A giant word hard-cuts in at the center, in display 200 px, cream, on a dark plate (`--ink` at 85 % behind it): **OWNERSHIP.**, then **BORROWING.**, then **LIFETIMES.**
  - Each comes with a white flash (opacity 0.35 → 0 over 150 ms), a 10 px shake, and a scale drift 1.00 → 1.04.
  - Each word replaces the previous one.
- **14.0–15.5:** the rhythm feels faster. The cards keep landing, the shake builds (amplitude 2 → 8 px), and the camera rotates −1° to +1°.
- **@breath 15.5–16.0:** hard cut to pure black, with one tiny molten spark breathing in the center (radius 4 → 10 px with a glow). Total silence in the music.

## 4. Engine contract (`engine.js`). Scenes are written against this.

`index.html` loads, in order:
- `fonts/fonts.css`;
- `../site/assets/cast-art.css`, `../site/assets/rustopia.css`;
- `../site/assets/cast-art.js`, `../site/assets/rustopia.js`;
- `engine.js`;
- then `scenes/s1-invisible.js`, `s2-wall.js`, `s3-ignite.js`, `s4-ownership.js`, `s5-montage.js`, `s6-forge.js`, `s7-journey.js`, `s8-ship.js` (a missing file must not break the page);
- then `V.boot()`.

The stage is a fixed 1920×1080 `#stage` with `overflow: hidden`. All CSS animations and transitions are disabled globally:

```css
*, *::before, *::after { animation: none !important; transition: none !important; }
```

**The `window.V` API:**

```js
V.W, V.H, V.FPS            // 1920, 1080, 60 (from timing.json)
V.cue                      // timing.json "cues" object; V.sections likewise
V.scene({ id, start, end, setup(layer), render(t, layer, T) })
   // Registers a scene. layer: a full-stage absolutely positioned <div class="scene s-<id>">.
   // setup(layer) runs once at boot and builds the DOM.
   // render(t, layer, T) runs for every frame with start <= T < end; t = T - start.
   // It MUST be stateless: it sets every animated property from t alone. Never accumulate.
   // The engine sets layer.style.display = "none" outside [start, end).
V.ease.linear | inQuad | outQuad | inOutQuad | inCubic | outCubic | inOutCubic |
       inExpo | outExpo | inOutExpo | inOutSine | outBack | outElastic
V.p(T, a, b, ease = V.ease.linear)   // eased progress of T across [a, b], clamped to 0..1
V.lerp(a, b, u); V.clamp(x, lo, hi); V.mixColor("#hex", "#hex", u) -> "rgb(...)"
V.rand(seed)                          // returns a seeded PRNG fn () -> [0, 1) (mulberry32)
V.set(el, { x, y, s, sx, sy, r, o, blur, origin })  // transform: translate(x,y) rotate(r deg) scale(...); opacity; filter blur(px)
V.type(el, text, u, { caret })        // shows the first round(u * len) chars; optional block caret
V.chars(el, text)                     // splits into <span class="ch"> per char; returns the array
V.shake(T, hits, amp = 8, decay = 0.35)  // -> {x, y} pixel offset from hit times (deterministic)
V.flash(T, times, dur = 0.15, peak = 1)  // -> opacity for flashes at given times
V.cast(id, opts) / V.boss(id, opts)    // HTML strings from OxCast.el / OxCast.boss
V.path(points)                         // SVG path "d" for a smooth curve through points (for ropes and links)
window.renderAt(T)                     // renders global time T synchronously (used by render.mjs)
window.__ready                         // a Promise that resolves after fonts, timing.json and every setup()
```

The engine also draws on top of every frame:
- the **film grain**: a pre-generated noise tile, offset per frame index, opacity 0.07;
- the **vignette**: a radial gradient.

**Preview URLs:**
- `index.html?t=12.5` renders one frame;
- `index.html?play&from=24` plays in real time with `out/music.wav`, if it exists.

**Tools:** all run from the repo root, `node promo/<tool>`.
- `serve.mjs`: a tiny static server for the repo root; picks a free port, or uses `PORT`.
- `render.mjs [--from s] [--to s] [--fps 60] [--workers 4] [--out promo/out/frames]` renders JPEG frames (quality 92) with Playwright Chromium (`/opt/node22/lib/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs`), in parallel workers, and prints progress.
- `sheet.mjs <from> <to> <count> <out.png> [--w 384]` makes a contact sheet of `count` evenly spaced frames with time labels, for review.
- `frame.mjs <T> <out.png>` renders a single full-size frame.
- `encode.sh` encodes the frames plus `out/music.wav` into `out/oxidize.mp4` with H.264 (CRF 17, `-preset slow`, yuv420p, `+faststart`) and AAC 256k. It also writes a 15-second teaser cut, `out/oxidize-teaser.mp4` (14.0–30.0 s), with a 0.5 s audio fade in and out.

## 5. The score (`music.py`)

The score is synthesized sample by sample in numpy, 48 kHz stereo, 16-bit WAV, written to `out/music.wav`, exactly 64.0 s. No samples or downloads. It is deterministic (seeded).

- **Tempo and key:** 120 BPM, 4/4. D minor; the main loop is **Dm | B♭ | F | C** (one chord per bar). The finale resolves to **D major**: B♭ → C → **D**, a Picardy lift.
- **Cues:** read `timing.json`. Every visual hit has a matching sound at the exact sample.

| bars (time) | section | arrangement |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 (0–8) | INVISIBLE | **Sub drone** on D1 (sine plus a faint 2nd harmonic, low-passed and slowly breathing). **Ticking hats**: short high-passed noise ticks on 8th notes, quiet and slightly swung, like a clock or compiler. **Glass plucks**, a sparse motif: A4 (0.75 s), D5 (2.0), F5 (3.0), E5 (4.5), A4 (6.0), each with a long delay tail. An **error stab** at @errorFlash: a dissonant low cluster (D2+E♭2+A♭2, saw, low-pass, 300 ms). |
| 5–8 (8–16) | THE WALL | The drone continues. **Pulse bass** on D2 8th notes, getting louder. An **error stab on every @errorCards hit**: short, distorted, alternating pitch, panned left and right. **Word hits** at @wordHits: a deep tom plus a sub drop plus a reversed swell into each.
